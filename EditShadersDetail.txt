Go to C/windows/regedit in the windows registry editor and go to "Computer\HKEY_CURRENT_USER\Software\Petroglyph\StarWars FOC\Profiles\Profile0\Player"

Find the files called, "ShaderDetail" and "WaterDetail" and set their values to 3 for "ShaderDetail" and 2 for "WaterDetail"
